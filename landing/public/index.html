How it works

Four steps from quote to payout.

01

Write the job

Janus turns the quote into a job card with a checklist. Both sides see the same list.

02

Hold the money

The customer approves a PayPal hold. The provider knows the money is there. The customer is not charged yet.

03

Check the evidence

The provider uploads photos and notes. Janus checks them against the checklist and builds an acceptance packet.

04

Decide

The customer accepts and the provider is paid. Or the customer rejects and the hold is released.
